QuickShare is a tiny, no-nonsense uploader for your phone.

Pick any file — a photo, a PDF, a video, a zip — and QuickShare uploads it to
your own Google Drive automatically, sets it to "anyone with the link can view",
and copies the shareable link to your clipboard. Done in one tap.

WHAT IT DOES

• Sign in with Google — access is limited to Drive, nothing else
• Choose any file type from your phone
• Automatic upload the moment you pick the file
• The file is made public (anyone with the link can view)
• The shareable link is copied to your clipboard and shown on screen
• Upload history saved on your phone — tap to copy a link again, or clear it
• Handles Google's permission expiry automatically, no errors

NO NONSENSE

• No account of its own, no server, no tracking, no ads
• Your file goes straight from your phone to your own Google Drive
• Nothing is stored anywhere except your local upload history
• Minimal white + black interface — just the buttons you need

PRIVACY

QuickShare has no backend. Your file is uploaded directly to your own Google
Drive using Google's official API. The only data kept on the device is a local
list of file names, links and timestamps.
